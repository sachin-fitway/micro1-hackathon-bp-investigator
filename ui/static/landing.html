Executive Summary

During an ecommerce_checkout process, the inventory_reserve step was skipped due to a legacy fast-path configuration. Payment authorization failed because no active reservation was in place.

Incident Timeline

  • 10:00:03Z inventory-service — Reservation skipped: legacy fast-path enabled c01-02
  • 10:00:05Z payment-gateway — Authorization failed: no active reservation c01-03

Detected Divergence

inventory_reserve · sequence_skip

Causal Chain

  1. inventory_reserve skipped (legacy fast-path)
  2. payment_authorize attempted without reservation
  3. Authorization failed — checkout aborted

Evidence

Log IDServiceMessage

c01-03payment-gatewayAuthorization failed: no active reservation

Recommended Remediation

  1. Investigate legacy fast-path conditions and disable if unintended
  2. Enforce reservation dependency before payment authorization
  3. Alert when inventory_reserve is skipped unexpectedly
